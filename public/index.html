Talk back

Licensed operators only
On-air talk-back keys your own transceiver. Transmit only on frequencies your licence covers, identify with your call sign, and never send a false distress call: it is a crime and diverts rescuers. Aviation, beacon and distress-guard frequencies are always blocked.
